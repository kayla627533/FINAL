JUST BE

by mikayla october

My project is a two-page comic titled “Just Be.” It follows the story of a young woman in a world where perception is everything. At every turn, she’s faced with piercing, judging eyes that stare at her no matter what. Despite not having any mouths, the eyes emit whispers that are judgements of the womans appearance, character, and pretty much everything about her. It eventually becomes too much, and she begins to feel isolated as a large, red eye peers above her, surrounding her in ‘noise.’ It isn’t until she realizes to find herself that her inner light shines within and blinds the eyes at the end.
